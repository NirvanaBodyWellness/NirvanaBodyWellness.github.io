Meet Mara

Mara Orozco is a passionate LMT dedicated to creating a safe and welcoming environment for everybody. After launching her bodywork career in Virginia back in 2023, she brought her talents to California, officially earning her state certification in 2025. Every session is fully customized and designed around what you need for the day, with a clear goal: helping you feel like your best self. Nothing brings this therapist more joy than tracking a client's progress over time—or hearing them peacefully drift off to sleep on her table! Looking ahead, Mara is eager to expand her toolkit and knowledge into myofascial release, manual lymphatic drainage, yoga, and personal training. She is a firm believer in using strength training and stretching as empowering self-care tools, which she loves sharing with her clients. When she isn't in the studio, she can be found pampering her two beloved cats, diving into a good book, immersing herself in cozy games, or binging a great TV show. Ready to reset, unwind, and move with more ease? Mara's table is waiting for you!
